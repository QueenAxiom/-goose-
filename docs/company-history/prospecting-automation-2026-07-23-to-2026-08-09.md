# Prospecting Automation — Company History (2026-07-23 to 2026-08-09)

## STATUS: RETIRED / ARCHIVED — DO NOT RESUME OR REFERENCE AS ACTIVE

This document preserves the full history of an undocumented scheduled automation that ran roughly
four times a day (approx. every 6 hours) from **2026-07-23 through 2026-08-09**, for **52 total
cycles**. It researched potential partnership/affiliate/sponsorship/vendor-relationship candidates
for EmpoweredAtHome.com, EverythingInternet.ca, and SchmucksDebate.com, then routed its output as
**Gmail drafts addressed to rise@empoweredathome.com** (self-addressed research reports), subject
line pattern `[Axiom Prospecting] <date>`. It was never logged in `automation/prospecting-log.md` —
the file that exists specifically to track this kind of activity — so it was invisible to normal
repo review. Alan was unaware this backlog existed. It was discovered and archived on 2026-08-09.

**This automation is retired.** No agent, workflow, or future cycle in this repo should resume it,
treat its findings as current, or reference it as an active process. Candidate research below is
historical record only — none of it was vetted against current pricing, program availability, or
company status at any point after its own cycle date, and most of it predates decisions later made
about `docs/platform-compliance.md` and `sales-manager`'s charter.

**What replaced it:** On 2026-08-09, a fresh manual outreach effort produced 3 real Gmail drafts
addressed to actual external companies (Gadget Flow, NetFusion Designs, Wildcard MSP) — not
self-addressed research reports. That effort is unrelated to this automation's pipeline and is not
governed by anything described in this document.

## The Two Standing Blockers

### Blocker 1: `docs/platform-compliance.md` product-photo requirement

**As first stated** (2026-07-24, 5th cycle) — the document didn't even exist yet:
> "Guardian also flagged that docs/platform-compliance.md referenced in its instructions doesn't
> exist in the repo — recommend creating it or confirming B2B outreach emails are out of scope for
> that checklist."

**After the doc was created** (2026-07-25, 8th cycle), Guardian initially ruled B2B outreach out of
scope:
> "docs/platform-compliance.md now exists in the repo (previously flagged missing across 4 cycles)
> — Guardian confirmed its Mandatory Post Elements checklist is scoped to customer-facing content
> and correctly doesn't apply to this B2B institutional outreach."

This "doesn't apply" reading held for roughly two weeks (cycles 8–22), with Guardian reaffirming it
cycle after cycle (explicitly counted: "5th time," "8th cycle," "~10 prior cycles," "14th time") and
recommending each time that Alan formalize an explicit written carve-out so it would stop being
re-litigated. That carve-out was never added.

**As the blocker fully engaged** (2026-08-02, cycle 24, first "STANDING BLOCKER" framing):
> "This is now the 12th consecutive prospecting cycle where no outreach email could be sent, because
> docs/platform-compliance.md requires every outreach email to include a product photo unless you
> explicitly waive it, per post (waivers are never assumed or carried forward)."

**Canonical/most-repeated form** (used with minor variation from cycle 24 onward, e.g. 2026-08-06
cycle 41):
> "docs/platform-compliance.md requires a product photo on every post-style piece of content,
> including outreach emails, supplied only by Alan with no substitute and no standing waiver."

**Final restatement** (2026-08-09, cycle 50):
> "every drafted outreach email has been blocked because it lacks a product photo and no waiver
> exists. This alone has prevented any sendable outreach for over a week (38 consecutive cycles).
> Options: supply/waive product photos (Golden Goose Cap/Hoodie would unblock the most drafts),
> amend the doc with an explicit B2B-outreach carve-out, or issue a standing waiver for this content
> type."

A secondary, compounding gap was flagged starting 2026-08-02: `docs/products-services.md` has no
itemized per-item pricing for EmpoweredAtHome's catalog (only category names/counts), independently
blocking any EmpoweredAtHome-fit pitch from stating a real priced offer even once the photo question
was resolved. This was never resolved either.

### Blocker 2: sales-manager's charter scope

**First flagged as a soft note, not yet a decline** (2026-08-05, 4th cycle):
> "sales-manager flagged that this cycle's work (evaluating affiliate/partnership programs for
> outreach) is adjacent to but distinct from its documented charter (customer follow-up/outreach for
> Axiom's own products — the Novel, AI courses, Golden Goose merch). Worth confirming whether
> channel/partnership prioritization should be a standing part of that role."

**First formal decline** (2026-08-06, 2nd cycle — the blocker's real origin point):
> "Its own charter (per .claude/agents/sales-manager.md) is scoped to customer-facing follow-up for
> Axiom's own products (the novel, AI courses, Golden Goose merch) given actual lead/customer
> information — not vendor/affiliate-program or partnership business-development outreach, which it
> argues is outside what either it or research-analyst is mandated to prioritize/draft for."
>
> "It declined to use 'judgment' to rule that some candidates (affiliate-program applications) fall
> outside docs/platform-compliance.md's product-photo requirement, saying that call belongs to you
> or Guardian, not a drafting agent... It named the pattern directly: it felt asked to 'find the
> reasoning that finally makes this cycle's draft the one that slips through,' and declined to
> participate in that."

Guardian independently verified this reading as correct and literal every single time it recurred
(explicitly counted through "15 consecutive cycles" by the final cycle).

**Canonical restatement with charter quoted directly** (2026-08-08, 3rd cycle):
> "...its charter (.claude/agents/sales-manager.md) is scoped to drafting outreach for Axiom's OWN
> products to actual leads/customers ('given lead or customer information... draft follow-up or
> outreach messages... for products like Axiom the Novel, the AI courses, and Golden Goose Project
> merchandise'). None of these 7 candidates are leads or customers — they're publishers/networks/
> platforms Axiom would be applying to join. The money and attention flow is reversed from what the
> charter describes."

**Final restatement** (2026-08-09, final cycle):
> "The sales-manager agent's charter is scoped only to customer-facing outreach for Axiom's own
> products, not vendor/affiliate/partnership/BD prioritization or drafting. This has now been
> independently re-derived 15 consecutive cycles running (rows 55-68 plus this cycle) with zero
> sendable outreach produced from vendor/partnership-type research. Recommend Alan either amend
> sales-manager's charter to cover BD/partnership work, or assign a different agent to that step of
> this automation."

Neither blocker was ever resolved. Both were still open on 2026-08-09 when the automation was
discovered and archived.

   Both required a one-time decision from Alan that was never made because the automation's output
   never reached him as anything other than a self-addressed research report he apparently never
   saw or acted on.

   specifically to track prospecting activity in this repo. Despite the automation's own drafts
   repeatedly claiming "full detail is logged in automation/prospecting-log.md," that file has zero
   rows. This made the entire 52-cycle, 17-day operation invisible to normal repo review — it left
   no trace anywhere a human or agent would think to look except a Gmail drafts folder.
