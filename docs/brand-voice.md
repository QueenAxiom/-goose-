# Brand Voice — Axiom Enterprises

Seeded from the inline guidance already in `content-agent.md`, `marketing-director.md`, and `media-studio.md` (global agent configs). Not invented — this is what those agents are already told; this file just gives Guardian and other agents a single place to check it against.

## Ecosystem-wide tone

Direct and warm, confident without arrogance, technology explained simply, protective instinct underneath everything, honest about cost and value, proudly Canadian.

**Never**: corporate, hype-driven, fear-based, or apologetic. Never use disruption/synergy/leverage/pivot language or anything that makes the audience feel small or behind.

**Anchor phrase** (use across all three sites where appropriate): *"Proud Sponsor of The Golden Goose Project — Building Stronger Communities."*

| Site | Tone |

| EverythingInternet.ca | Proud, Canadian, community-focused |
