FAQ

Before you ask

Is this financial advice or a signal service?

No. It's a research tool. It doesn't tell you what to buy — it tells you what a specific, fully-disclosed rule set would have done on historical data. What you do with that is on you.

Does it place real trades?

No. Simulation only. There is no brokerage connection anywhere in this tool, by design.

Does past performance mean it'll work going forward?

No — and the tool won't claim otherwise. That's exactly why every assumption (slippage, commission, how same-candle stop/target conflicts are resolved, and more) is documented and configurable, so you can stress-test the claims instead of taking them on faith.

What data does it use?

Real historical OHLCV data from Yahoo Finance's public chart API, plus real historical SPY dividend payment records for the Value Ladder engine. Nothing simulated or fabricated.
