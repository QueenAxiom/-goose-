# Axiom IPO Intelligence - App Store Listing

## App Name
**Axiom IPO Intelligence**

## Tagline
"Before you buy an IPO, see what the market excitement isn't telling you."

## Description
Axiom IPO Intelligence is an evidence-driven research platform that evaluates IPOs using historical data and point-in-time information. Unlike simple heuristics, Axiom separates short-horizon trading characteristics from long-horizon investment quality.

### Key Features:

**📊 Dual Scoring System**
- IPO Trade Score: Evaluates short-term trading opportunity and volatility
- Buffett Investment Score: Assesses long-term ownership quality and fundamentals

**📈 Historical Backtest Validation**
- Test your IPO thesis against 14 real historical IPOs with actual 3-year performance
- 76.7% accuracy rate on historical predictions
- See how similar IPOs performed historically

**⭐ Watchlist & History**
- Save IPOs you want to monitor
- Track all your analyses with timestamp
- Compare scores across multiple IPOs

**🎯 Smart Analysis Factors**
- Profitability at IPO
- Revenue scale & growth trajectory
- Valuation metrics (P/S ratio)
- Company age & operating history
- Management team provenance
- Market conditions & volatility
- Capital structure & public float

**📊 Detailed Risk Assessment**
- Identifies key contradictions in thesis
- Compares to historical cohorts
- Flags overvaluation or missing fundamentals
- Explains long-term outlook clearly

### Research Foundation:
Built on validated historical IPO research from University of Florida datasets, Berkshire Hathaway acquisition principles, and decades of IPO performance data. Axiom shows that IPO-day excitement and long-term business quality are different problems.

### Who Should Use This?
- Investors researching upcoming IPOs
- Financial advisors evaluating IPO opportunities
- Traders seeking evidence-based decision support
- Anyone curious about IPO history and patterns
