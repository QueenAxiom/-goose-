## Capabilities and Constraints

Products and ruled prices, as set by the owner:

| Product | Price |
|---|---|

| Website Receptionist | $499 setup plus $299/month |

These prices are ruled. Do not change, round, or add to them.

Business in a Bot is licensed to business owners only. Its page says "For business owners" near the top, and its application form requires the applicant to name their business.

Free door (owner, 2026-11-27): Business in a Bot is free to use for any registered business that gives its business registration number. It is sold commercially through the paid doors. Visitors without a registration get a "Need to register your business? Click here" link. Open: what the free door includes compared with the paid doors, and where "Click here" goes (owner says Axiom builds it; the draft page links to Alberta's business name registration page for now).

Confirmed by the owner (2026-11-27), and safe to use on pages:
- All prices are CAD, GST included.
- Financed: 10% down, and the rest paid from 1% of gross sales.
- Royalty: 2% of gross sales for life, nothing up front.
- $197/month with the 14-day trial is its own door, not an add-on.
- The $1,970/year option is retired. Do not use it.

## Brand Commitments

- The owner's wording is kept as written. Ask before any creative change to their copy.
- Copy is short, direct, Grade 10 reading level, no filler.
- Voice for this site per `docs/brand-voice.md`: proud, Canadian, community-focused.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, customer names, or results have been supplied. Do not make any up.

## Product Principles

1. Every page sells a product. A page that doesn't move a buyer toward one doesn't belong.
2. Write for a small business owner with little time: plain words, the point first.
3. Show the ruled price exactly as set.
4. Use only real claims. With no proof on hand, state what the product does instead of inventing results.
