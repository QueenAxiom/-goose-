## Brand Commitments

- The owner's wording is kept as written. Ask before any creative change to their copy.
- Copy is short, direct, Grade 10 reading level, no filler.
- Voice for this site per `docs/brand-voice.md`: proud, Canadian, community-focused.

## Evidence on Hand

No testimonials, customer names, or results have been supplied. Do not make any up.

## Product Principles

1. Every page sells a product. A page that doesn't move a buyer toward one doesn't belong.
2. Write for a small business owner with little time: plain words, the point first.
3. Show the ruled price exactly as set.
4. Use only real claims. With no proof on hand, state what the product does instead of inventing results.
