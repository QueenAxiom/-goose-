Privacy Policy

EverythingInternet.ca — operated by Axiom Enterprises · Last updated: 2026-08-17 (draft)

Axiom Enterprises ("Axiom," "we," "us," or "our") operates EverythingInternet.ca (the "Site"), a directory of Canadian technology companies. This Privacy Policy describes in detail what personal information we collect, how and why we collect it, who we share it with, how long we keep it, and the rights you have over it. We comply with the federal Personal Information Protection and Electronic Documents Act ("PIPEDA") and, where applicable, provincial privacy legislation including Quebec's Act Respecting the Protection of Personal Information in the Private Sector as amended by Law 25. By using the Site, submitting a listing, or otherwise providing us information, you acknowledge this Policy.

1. Scope of This Policy

This Policy applies to personal information collected through the Site, through direct correspondence with us (email, phone, RingCentral SMS), through the listing-purchase and checkout process, and through any account you create to manage your listing. It does not apply to the practices of companies listed in our directory — each listed company is a separate, independent business, and their own websites are governed by their own privacy policies, not this one.

2. Information We Collect

Information you provide directly: business/company name, business description and category, business contact name, email address and phone number, website and social links, logo and other submitted images, and any information included in correspondence you send us.

Payment information: when you purchase a listing, payment card and billing details are collected and processed directly by our payment processor (Stripe). We do not receive, store, or have access to your full card number, CVV, or other full payment credentials — we receive only confirmation of payment, transaction identifiers, and limited billing metadata (e.g., last four digits, billing name) needed for our own accounting records.

Account information: if you create an account to manage your listing, we collect a username/email and a securely hashed password, along with your listing management history (edits, renewals, correspondence with support).

Automatically collected information: standard web usage data such as IP address, browser type and version, device type, operating system, referring/exit pages, pages visited, and timestamps, collected via server-log-level web analytics. Confirmed 2026-08-17 directly against the live site: no third-party analytics or tracking script (e.g. Google Analytics, Meta Pixel, Clarity, Hotjar) is currently installed.

Cookies and similar technologies: see Section 8 below.

Information from third parties: where a listing is submitted or updated on a company's behalf by a third party (e.g., an employee, marketing agency, or an Axiom sales representative conducting outreach), we may receive that company's business contact information from that third party rather than directly from the listed company.

Inferred and aggregated information: we may derive aggregated, de-identified statistics (e.g., "X% of visitors browse the fintech category") from the information above. Aggregated data that cannot reasonably be used to identify an individual or company is not considered personal information under this Policy.

3. How We Use Information

We use the personal information described above to: create, publish, update, and maintain your directory listing; verify listing submissions and prevent fraudulent or duplicate listings; process payment for listing purchases and renewals; communicate with you about your listing, your account, or a support request; send transactional communications (receipts, listing-status notices); send marketing or promotional communications where you have consented, in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation ("CASL") — see Section 9; operate, maintain, secure, and improve the Site, including aggregated, non-identifying analytics; detect, investigate, and prevent fraud, abuse, and security incidents; comply with legal obligations, including tax, accounting, and regulatory recordkeeping; and enforce our Terms and Conditions. We do not sell personal information to third parties, and we do not use your information for any purpose materially different from those listed here without seeking fresh consent.

4. Legal Basis for Collection (PIPEDA)

Under PIPEDA, we collect, use, and disclose personal information only with knowledge and consent, except where PIPEDA permits collection without consent (for example, to comply with a legal or regulatory requirement, or in connection with an investigation of a suspected breach of an agreement or law). Consent may be express (e.g., checking a box to receive marketing emails) or implied by the context of the interaction (e.g., providing your contact email when you submit a listing implies consent for us to use it to communicate about that listing). You may withdraw consent at any time, subject to legal or contractual restrictions and reasonable notice, by contacting us as described in Section 15.

5. How We Share Information

Service providers and processors: we share information with third parties who perform services on our behalf, and only to the extent necessary for them to perform that function:

— Payment processing: Stripe, and, once self-service vendor payouts are active, Stripe Connect, governed additionally by Stripe's own privacy policy.
— Hosting and infrastructure: GoDaddy.
— Email and communications: our email provider (Google Workspace/Gmail) and RingCentral for SMS/voice correspondence.
— Automation and workflow tooling: n8n (our internal automation platform), used to route listing submissions and outreach — n8n does not independently sell or share your data.
— Analytics: none currently in use.

Public disclosure via the directory itself: the core function of the Site is to publish your business information (company name, description, category, logo, contact/website links) publicly. This is the expected, intended use of listing information and is not a "sharing" event requiring separate consent beyond your purchase of a listing.

Legal and safety disclosures: we may disclose information where required by law, court order, or governmental request, or where we believe in good faith that disclosure is necessary to protect our rights, your safety, or the safety of others, or to investigate fraud or security issues.

Business transfers: if Axiom Enterprises is involved in a merger, acquisition, financing, or sale of assets, personal information may be disclosed or transferred as part of that transaction, subject to standard confidentiality protections. We will notify affected individuals where required by law.

With your consent: we may share information for any other purpose with your explicit consent.

6. International Data Transfers

Some of our service providers (including certain payment, hosting, or analytics infrastructure) may store or process information outside of Canada, including in the United States. Where personal information is transferred outside Canada, it may become subject to the laws of that jurisdiction, including lawful access requests by foreign governments or authorities. We select service providers that maintain privacy and security safeguards appropriate to the sensitivity of the information involved.

7. Data Retention

We retain listing and account information for as long as your listing remains active on the Site, and for a reasonable period afterward for legal, accounting, tax, and dispute-resolution purposes (generally no more than seven years from the end of the business relationship, consistent with standard Canadian recordkeeping practice, unless a longer period is required by law). Correspondence is retained for as long as reasonably necessary to resolve the matter it relates to and to maintain a support history. Where information is no longer needed for these purposes, we take reasonable steps to delete, destroy, or anonymize it.

8. Cookies and Tracking Technologies

Cookies are small text files placed on your device that help a site function and help us understand how it's used. Confirmed 2026-08-17 directly against the live site: the only cookies currently set are WordPress/WooCommerce's own strictly-necessary session cookies (cart contents, login state) — there is no analytics, advertising, or third-party tracking cookie in use. If that changes (an analytics tool or ad pixel is added), this section and a cookie-consent mechanism will be updated before that tool goes live, not after.

Strictly necessary (in use): WordPress/WooCommerce session cookies for cart persistence, checkout, and account login. These cannot be disabled without breaking core site functions.
Analytics/performance: none currently in use.
Functional: none beyond the strictly-necessary session cookies above.
Advertising/marketing: none — the Site runs no third-party advertising or ad-tracking pixels.

You can control or delete cookies through your browser settings; blocking strictly necessary cookies will prevent checkout and login from working.

9. Marketing Communications and CASL

Where we send you promotional or marketing messages (as opposed to transactional messages about your existing listing or order), we do so in accordance with Canada's Anti-Spam Legislation. Marketing messages will include a functional unsubscribe mechanism, and we will honour opt-out requests promptly. You may also withdraw consent to marketing communications at any time by contacting us using the details in Section 15. Withdrawing marketing consent does not affect transactional communications necessary to service your existing listing.

10. Do-Not-Track Signals

Some browsers offer a "Do Not Track" signal. There is no single industry-standard interpretation of these signals, and the Site does not currently respond differently to browsers that send them. We will revisit this position if a common standard for responding to such signals is adopted.

11. Data Security

We use reasonable administrative, technical, and physical safeguards designed to protect personal information against unauthorized access, alteration, disclosure, or destruction, including relying on the security practices of our payment processor and hosting provider for the data they handle on our behalf. No method of transmission or storage is completely secure, and we cannot guarantee absolute security. If we become aware of a breach of security safeguards involving personal information that creates a real risk of significant harm, we will notify affected individuals and, where required, the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, in accordance with PIPEDA's breach-reporting requirements.

12. Your Privacy Rights

Subject to limited exceptions provided by law, you have the right to: request access to the personal information we hold about you; request correction of inaccurate or incomplete information; request deletion of your personal information; withdraw previously given consent; and request a copy of your information in a portable format. Quebec residents have additional rights under Law 25, including the right to request de-indexing of certain information and the right to be informed of automated decision-making that significantly affects them (see Section 13). To exercise any of these rights, contact us using the details in Section 15. We will respond within the time limits required by applicable law and may request information to verify your identity before actioning a request.

13. Automated Decision-Making

We do not currently use automated decision-making or profiling that produces legal or similarly significant effects on individuals (for example, we do not use automated systems to approve or deny listings without human review). If this changes, we will update this Policy to describe the logic involved and your rights in relation to it, as required under Quebec Law 25 and other applicable law.

14. Children's Privacy

This Site is a business-to-business directory intended for use by business owners, employees, and professionals. It is not directed at, and is not intended for use by, children under the age of 13, and we do not knowingly collect personal information from children. If we become aware that we have inadvertently collected information from a child, we will take reasonable steps to delete it.

We may update this Policy from time to time to reflect changes in our practices, technology, legal requirements, or for other operational reasons. The "Last updated" date at the top of this page reflects the most recent revision.
